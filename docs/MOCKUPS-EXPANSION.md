# Mockup expansion


## Expanded collection and 3D previews

The collection now has 16 scenes: seven for JM Marketing and three each for Duara Schools, iClear and Sidi Safari. The ten additional scenes were created using built-in imagegen, then saved as full-size JPEGs and thumbnails alongside the originals.

Eight scenes have an optional interactive WebGL concept view: JM's card, mug and bottle; iClear's card and bottle; Sidi's card and mug; and Duara's mug. These are procedurally modelled meshes (a hollow mug and handle, a bottle and cap, or a thin card) with the existing identity assets on their surfaces. They are simplified concept previews, not exact replicas or production CAD. Apparel and bags use photographs only.

The viewer uses the existing OGL dependency and loads only after choosing **Explore in 3D**. It renders on interaction/resize instead of running a permanent animation loop, caps pixel density, releases GPU resources on unmount, and falls back to the photo if WebGL or a logo fails. Drag, keyboard arrows, zoom controls and reset are supported. `?view=3d` combines with the brand and category filters.
